Founding Growth Partnership · 2027

Help us build the organisation behind the ideas.

Smile Optimistic Studio CIC is a young UK social enterprise developing creative resources that support children and families through difficult experiences.

In our first year, we have developed our first physical product, Hospital Joy Toolkit, alongside professional guidance materials and an emerging network of healthcare and community contacts.

We are now seeking a strategic founding growth partner to help us establish the professional capacity, independent evaluation and sustainable operating model needed for our next stage.

This is an opportunity to support not just one project, but the long-term development of a social enterprise designed to create and deliver lasting community benefit.

Let's explore a partnership.

We welcome conversations with individuals, businesses and organisations interested in supporting the next stage of Smile Optimistic Studio.
