2. What you are paying for

Fund a Toolkit — £25 per set

Your payment funds one Hospital Joy Toolkit: a boxed set of 26 illustrated A–Z emotional support cards for children aged 4–6, supplied with an information card.

The Toolkit is not sent to you. Smile Optimistic Studio CIC arranges delivery to a participating hospital, hospice or family-support organisation for distribution to a child or family in its care.

You may fund more than one Toolkit in a single payment.

The £25 price includes production, packaging and delivery to the receiving organisation. There are no additional delivery charges.

Support the Next Generation

Where offered, this is a separate, optional voluntary payment towards developing future Hospital Joy Toolkit collections for children aged 7–10 and 11–15.

No product is supplied in return for this separate payment. It is not a charitable donation and is not eligible for Gift Aid.

This optional payment is not required to fund a Toolkit.

3. Our commitment

Every funded Hospital Joy Toolkit will be supplied through a participating hospital, hospice or family-support organisation that has agreed in writing to receive Toolkits.

We arrange deliveries as participating organisations confirm their capacity and requirements.

Some Toolkits may arrive before Christmas, while others will be delivered afterwards. Funding a Toolkit does not guarantee delivery before Christmas or allocation to a particular child, family or organisation.

We aim to complete delivery of all Toolkits funded through this campaign by 31 March 2027.

We are committed to identifying appropriate receiving organisations for every funded Toolkit, subject to their written agreement, and maintaining accurate records of Toolkit distribution.

If we are unable to arrange delivery of a funded Toolkit, we will contact the purchaser to discuss an appropriate resolution, including a refund where applicable.

4. How distribution works

We supply Toolkits only to hospitals, hospices and family-support organisations that have agreed in writing to receive them.

Receiving organisations independently identify suitable children or families and arrange distribution according to their own procedures.

Parents, carers and private individuals cannot apply directly to Smile Optimistic Studio CIC to receive a funded Toolkit through this campaign.

We do not arrange direct delivery to individual children or families.

We do not require children's names, medical information or family contact details for the purpose of distributing funded Toolkits.

5. Updates on Toolkit distribution

We will keep a record of the number of Toolkits funded and delivered to participating organisations.

Campaign supporters will receive a personalised thank-you email on 29 December 2026.

We may publish general updates about Toolkit distribution, including the names of receiving organisations where they have agreed to be named.

Individual delivery notifications are not guaranteed. Delivery confirmation relates to the receiving organisation, not distribution to an individual child or family.

If you have a question about a Toolkit you have funded, you can contact us using the email address provided in these terms.

We do not share supporters' personal details with receiving organisations unless authorised or otherwise permitted by law.

6. Where the proceeds go

Each £25 payment covers a funded Toolkit, including production, packaging and delivery to a participating organisation.

After covering the costs of fulfilling funded Toolkits and relevant campaign expenses, proceeds support the development of Hospital Joy Toolkit collections for children aged 7–10 and 11–15.

Our long-term goal is to develop age-appropriate emotional support resources for children throughout their medical journeys.

7. About the Toolkit

Hospital Joy Toolkit is a creative emotional support resource intended for use by parents, carers and professionals.

It is designed to encourage communication, emotional expression and supportive interaction with children.

It does not replace clinical, medical or psychological care, or professional safeguarding procedures.
