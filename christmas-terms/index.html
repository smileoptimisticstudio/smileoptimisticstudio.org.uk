2. What you are paying for

Fund a Toolkit — £25 per set

Your payment funds one Hospital Joy Toolkit: a boxed set of 26 illustrated A–Z emotional support cards for children aged 4–6, supplied with an information card.

The Toolkit is not sent to you. We arrange delivery to a participating hospital, hospice or family-support organisation for distribution to a child or family in its care.

You may fund more than one Toolkit in a single payment.

The £25 price includes production, packaging and delivery to the receiving organisation. There are no additional delivery charges.

Support the Next Generation

Where offered, this is an optional voluntary payment towards developing future Hospital Joy Toolkit collections for children aged 7–10 and 11–15.

No product is supplied in return for this separate payment. It is not a charitable donation and is not eligible for Gift Aid.

3. Our commitment

Every funded Hospital Joy Toolkit will be supplied to a participating hospital, hospice or family-support organisation for distribution to a child or family in its care.

Toolkits will be delivered as participating organisations confirm their needs. Some may arrive before Christmas, while others will be delivered afterwards.

We aim to complete delivery of all Toolkits funded through this campaign by 31 March 2027.

We are committed to finding an appropriate receiving organisation for every funded Toolkit and keeping customers informed about its delivery.

4. How distribution works

We supply Toolkits only to hospitals, hospices and family-support organisations that have agreed to receive them.

These organisations independently identify suitable families and arrange distribution.

We do not request or receive children's names, medical information or family contact details.

5. Telling you where your Toolkit went

Once your Toolkit has been delivered to a participating organisation, we will email you to confirm its delivery.

Where the organisation has agreed to be named, we will tell you its name. Otherwise, we will provide a general description, such as a children's hospice in Greater Manchester.

We do not share your personal details with the receiving organisation unless you have authorised us to do so or another lawful basis applies.

Delivery confirmation means the Toolkit has reached the organisation. Individual distribution to families is arranged by that organisation.

6. Where the proceeds go

Each £25 payment covers a funded Toolkit, including production, packaging and delivery.

After covering the costs of fulfilling funded Toolkits and relevant campaign expenses, proceeds support the development of Hospital Joy Toolkit collections for children aged 7–10 and 11–15.

Our long-term goal is to develop age-appropriate emotional support resources for children throughout their medical journeys.

7. About the Toolkit

Hospital Joy Toolkit is a creative emotional support resource intended for use by parents, carers and professionals.

It does not replace clinical, medical or psychological care or professional safeguarding procedures.
