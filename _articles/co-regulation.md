A child is not born able to manage strong emotions alone. At first, they learn to do it together with an adult — through a voice, a presence, predictability, simple words and the chance to choose.

This is called **co-regulation**.

In hospital the need does not disappear. Fear, pain, uncertainty and the loss of control can make it stronger.

Yet the adult beside the child is often exhausted and frightened too.

This is the small gap the Hospital Joy Toolkit tries to account for. A card should not ask a parent to invent the right words in a moment of stress. It should help them take the next small step together with their child.
