A child can be angry. Can be afraid. Can say: "I don't want to."

An adult can answer: "I understand you're angry" — and still keep a boundary that has to stay.

It is an important distinction: **the feeling has every right to exist; the behaviour can still have limits.**

In hospital, parents find it especially hard to hold both at once — compassion for their child, and the need to get through something that cannot be avoided.

That is why the Hospital Joy Toolkit is built around more than activities. Its aim is to support child and adult inside the interaction itself: noticing the feeling, offering a choice where one is possible, lowering the load, and not turning support into one more demand.
