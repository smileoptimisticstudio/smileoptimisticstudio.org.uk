What you receive

One set, one feeling at a time

Each set contains 26 cards, one for every letter of the alphabet, plus a title card. Every card has two sides:

  • Front — for the child. An illustration and the name of a feeling.
  • Back — for the adult. Written for parents and carers to read aloud together with the child.

Printed at A6 on 350gsm card with matt lamination on both sides and rounded corners — chosen to be comfortable to hold and easy to read without glare.

How each card works

The same clear structure on every card

  1. Name it. A short opening that names the feeling and tells the child it is normal to feel this way.
  2. Say. Words the adult can use with the child.
  3. Do. Simple actions to try together.
  4. What this does. A brief explanation for the adult of why it helps.
  5. If that feels like too much right now. A smaller version for days when energy is low.
  6. Remember. A short closing line to hold on to.

Some cards also include an extra idea. The child always chooses — the cards are designed to be child-led, never imposed.

Development and review

How the Toolkit was developed

  • Lived experience. Lada Barsukova, lived experience consultant, reviewed the cards for relevance to children's real hospital experience.
  • Health Play Specialists. Health Play Specialists reviewed the cards and gave feedback, which has been addressed in the current edition.
  • Independent professional review. The 4–6 set is currently under independent professional review. Results will be published here when the review is complete.

This resource does not replace clinical psychological support.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What age is the Toolkit for?

The current set is designed for children aged 4–6. Sets for ages 7–10 and 11–15 are in development.

Who reads the cards?

The back of each card is written for a parent, carer or member of staff to read aloud with the child. The child chooses which card to use.

Can Health Play Specialists use it on the ward?

Yes. The Toolkit is designed to be used within child-led, developmentally appropriate play practice.

Has the Toolkit been professionally reviewed?

Health Play Specialists contributed to its development and their feedback has been addressed. An independent professional review of the 4–6 set is in progress, and its outcome will be shared here.

Does it replace psychological support?

No. The Toolkit supports everyday conversations about feelings. It does not replace clinical psychological support.

Can we receive a free sample set?

Free physical samples are not provided. For professional evaluation, we can share a digital overview of the cards and supporting documentation.
