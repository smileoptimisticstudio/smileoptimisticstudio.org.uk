What your gift provides

Small cards for difficult moments.

Hospital Joy Toolkit is a boxed set of 26 illustrated A–Z emotional support cards for children aged 4–6 experiencing hospital care and medical treatment.

Each card offers an adult gentle words and simple activities to share with a child at the child's pace. The child can choose whether and how to take part.

A practical resource for connection, not a replacement for medical or psychological care.

Questions & transparency

Good to know

Will the Toolkit reach a child before Christmas?

Not necessarily. Funded sets are delivered to approved partner organisations by our target date of 31 March 2027, and the organisations arrange distribution to families.

Can I fund more than one Toolkit?

Yes. Each set costs £25 and you can choose up to 99 Toolkits in Stripe checkout.

How can I follow the impact?

Our website counter shows how many Toolkits have been confirmed delivered to approved partner organisations. We update it monthly and it remains on the website after Christmas. You will also receive a personal thank-you email on 29 December 2026.

Can a family request a free Toolkit directly from SOS CIC?

No. Funded Toolkits are supplied only through verified partner organisations with written distribution agreements. We do not take individual applications or send funded Toolkits directly to families.

Is this a charitable donation? Can I claim Gift Aid?

No. Smile Optimistic Studio CIC is a Community Interest Company, not a registered charity. The £25 payment funds a Toolkit for distribution; Gift Aid does not apply.

What if I have a question about payment or fulfilment?

Email yuliia@smileoptimisticstudio.org.uk. Our campaign terms explain the arrangements and your statutory rights.
