The poster introduced the creative methodology behind the Hospital Joy Toolkit: a resource shaped by six years of family experience during long-term childhood cancer treatment, with a focus on emotional expression, connection and a child's freedom to choose.

It was built around a single card from the first set, L — Lonely, to show the structure every card shares: what to say, what to do together, what this does, a softer option for when it feels like too much, and one line to remember.

## Open to collaboration

The poster named three areas where the studio's lived experience meets a gap in research and resources:

- **Life in remission.** Most studies look at active treatment; far fewer at what comes after.
- **The resource gap.** Ready-made play resources are scarce while Health Play Specialists are stretched thin.
- **Being with a feeling, not only distracting from it.** Few tools help a child stay with a long, hard feeling.

## Professional contributions

Health Play Specialists have contributed professional knowledge and experience that informed the development of the cards. Their contribution is acknowledged in the Toolkit's Practice &amp; Training Guide.
