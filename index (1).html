Hospital Joy Toolkit

An Emotional Alphabet for Big Days in Hospital

26 illustrated cards, from A to Z, to help adults and children aged four to six talk about feelings during hospital care.

Hospital Joy Toolkit is an emotional communication resource, not a clinical or diagnostic tool. It does not replace clinical assessment, psychological care or professional advice.

Before you begin

  • Choose a card together, or let the child choose. There is no right order.
  • One card is enough. There is no need to finish an activity.
  • An adult can offer the cards. The child decides whether to carry on.
  • Follow the child's pace. Watching, listening, changing activity or stopping are all fine.
  • Never use the cards to delay clinical care or in place of professional advice.

Using a card

Show the child the picture side. The back is for you: what to Say, what to Do, and a gentler option, If that feels like too much right now.

When a child needs less

Just look at the picture together. Or name the feeling and leave it there. Or put the card away and come back another day.

Looking after the cards

The cards have a laminated surface. Follow your organisation's infection prevention and control guidance before use and between users.

Safety first

The Toolkit does not replace clinical assessment, safeguarding procedures or emergency care.

If a child is in pain, very distressed, has an urgent medical need, or tells you something that worries you, stop the activity and speak to their care team. Follow your local safeguarding procedures.
